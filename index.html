A little about me

Curious by nature.
Builder by choice.

I'm a 20-year-old Computer Science student currently in my fifth semester of a Diploma in Engineering at Thakurgaon Polytechnic Institute.

I'm not particularly obsessed with the clock. But when an idea gets stuck in my head, it keeps circling around until I understand it — or build something that solves it.

Technology became my choice because I love making things simpler. From small tools that remove friction from everyday life to Android applications built from an idea, I enjoy exploring how things work and turning curiosity into something useful.

Outside code, you'll often find me with a book. My first novel was Dutta by Sarat Chandra Chattopadhyay. I've read his works extensively, along with the Feluda collection. Reading, writing and observing the world quietly shape the way I build.
